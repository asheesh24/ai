Artificial Intelligence

Introduction to AI and intelligent systems

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with developing systems that can perform tasks requiring forms of reasoning, learning, perception or decision-making.

Major Areas of AI

🧠

Machine Learning

Systems learn patterns from data and use them to make predictions or decisions.

💬

Natural Language Processing

Computers process and understand human language.

👁️

Computer Vision

AI systems interpret images and visual information.

🤖

Robotics

AI can control robots and autonomous machines.

🔎

Expert Systems

Expert systems use knowledge and rules to solve specialized problems.

⚡

Reasoning

AI systems can use logical rules to derive conclusions.
