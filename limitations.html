Limitations

Challenges of rule-based fault diagnosis

Knowledge Acquisition

Building a complete knowledge base can require considerable domain knowledge.

Rule Explosion

A complex system may require a large number of rules.

Uncertainty

Simple rules may not adequately represent uncertain or incomplete information.

Maintenance

Rules need to be updated when system knowledge changes.

Limited Generalization

A rule-based system may not handle situations that were not represented in its rules.

Incorrect Rules

Incorrect knowledge can produce incorrect diagnostic conclusions.
